01 / COMPLIANCE

ADA accessible stalls & signage

Correctly sized accessible spaces, access aisles, van-accessible clearances, ground symbols and post signage — executed to inspection standard. Non-compliant lots are a liability; this is the work that removes it.

Highest-risk item on most lots
02 / COMPLIANCE

Fire lanes & code marking

Fire lane striping, no-parking zones and curb marking to local fire marshal and city requirements. We stripe it so it passes the first time.

Fire marshal ready
03 / STRIPING

Striping & restriping

New layouts, faded-line refreshes and full reconfigurations that maximize usable stalls and keep traffic moving through the property.

04 / NEW BUILD

New-construction layout

First-time striping worked from your site plan and built around the GC's schedule. We show up when the schedule says, not a week after.

05 / SITE SAFETY

Wheel stops, speed bumps & bollards

Supply and install of wheel stops, rubber speed bumps and humps, bollard posts, convex mirrors and custom signage.

06 / INTERIOR

Warehouse & floor striping

Aisle lanes, safety zones, dock markings and pedestrian walkways for warehouse and industrial facilities.

07 / STENCILS

Stencils, arrows & numbering

Directional arrows, stop bars, crosswalks, loading zones, reserved spaces, stall numbering and custom stencils to spec.

08 / SURFACE PREP

Power washing & sandblasting

Surface cleaning, sweeping, sandblasting and waterproofing — proper prep so the paint bonds and the lines last.

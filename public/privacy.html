Privacy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This page explains what 2 Way Striping LLC does with the information you send through our website forms.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form. The quote form asks for your name, phone, email (optional), property city, property type, the work you need, and project details. The job form asks for your name, phone, years of experience, city, equipment you have run, and experience and availability.

How we use it

To respond to your quote request or to follow up about a job. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who handles it

Form submissions are received and stored by Netlify, the company that hosts this site, and forwarded to us by email. Netlify processes the data only to provide that service.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to respond, to quote and complete work, or to consider a job applicant, and as required for our business records.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not currently use advertising cookies. If we add analytics, we will update this page.
